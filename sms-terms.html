Program description

MarrGwen Temperature Alerts is a private informational SMS program. Enrolled recipients receive automated text messages concerning high-temperature conditions at a monitored property.

Enrollment and consent

Enrollment requires the recipient's affirmative consent. The consent checkbox is unchecked by default. A recipient's mobile number is added only after the recipient actively requests enrollment and agrees to receive automated high-temperature alerts.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies according to temperature conditions and system status. Message and data rates may apply. MarrGwen does not charge a separate fee for the alert service.

Opt out

Reply STOP to any program message to stop future SMS messages. A confirmation message may be sent after the opt-out request. A recipient may also request removal by emailing Stuart@MarrGwen.com.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance or email Stuart@MarrGwen.com.

Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on the recipient's wireless service and network availability.

Privacy

Use of personal information is governed by the Privacy Policy. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Eligibility

The program is available only to authorized recipients associated with the monitored property. It is not a public subscription service.
