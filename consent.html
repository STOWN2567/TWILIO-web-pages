Recipient Consent Process

Temperature Monitoring is a private notification service for a privately owned residential property. This service is not open to the public, and there is no public website or online form for enrollment.

How Consent Is Obtained

Before any mobile number is added to the notification system, the account owner personally requests the recipient's explicit permission to receive automated SMS notifications regarding the monitored wine room. Only after the recipient has agreed is the mobile number entered into the monitoring system and SMS notifications enabled.

Message Types

Recipients receive only operational notifications, including:

  • Wine room temperature exceeds configured alert thresholds.
  • Wine room temperature returns to the acceptable operating range.
  • Monitoring system operational or status notifications.

This service does not send marketing, promotional, or advertising messages.

Opt-Out

Recipients may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP. They may re-enroll by replying START or obtain assistance by replying HELP.
