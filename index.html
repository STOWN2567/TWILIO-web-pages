Automated High-Temperature SMS Alerts

MarrGwen operates a private notification service that sends automated informational text messages when high-temperature conditions are detected at a monitored property.

Enrollment is voluntary. A mobile number is added only after the recipient actively requests enrollment and expressly agrees to receive these alerts.
